Spill The Beans

A personal publishing tool for one video channel.

Spill The Beans makes short animated explainer videos. This app is the small tool its creator uses to send finished videos from a computer to the channel's own TikTok account and to read how those videos perform.

What the app does

  • Uploads the creator's own finished videos to the creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator reviews each draft and publishes it by hand in the TikTok app.
  • Reads the public statistics of the creator's own videos (views, likes, comments, shares) and the account's follower count.

Who can use it

Only its creator, with their own TikTok account. It is not a service for other people and has no sign-up.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Maksym-Bahlai/spill-the-beans.
