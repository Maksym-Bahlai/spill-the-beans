Spill The Beans

Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy covers the Spill The Beans app ("the app"), a personal tool its developer uses with their own TikTok account. The app has no other users.

1. Data the app receives from TikTok

After the account holder approves it on TikTok's consent screen, the app receives:

  • the account's identifier (open ID) and display name, to confirm it is talking to the right account;
  • access and refresh tokens that let it act within the approved permissions;
  • the account's follower, following, like and video counts;
  • for each public video of the account: its ID, caption, post time, duration, link and its view, like, comment and share counts;
  • the processing status of videos the app uploaded.

The app does not read messages, contacts, comments' text, viewers' identities or any data about other TikTok users.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload the account holder's own videos as drafts and to show the account holder how those videos perform over time.

3. Where the data is kept

On the developer's own computer, in files readable only by that user account. The app has no server. The data is not sold, not shared and not sent to anyone except TikTok's own API when the app makes a request.

4. How long it is kept, and how to remove it

Tokens are kept until access is withdrawn or they expire. Statistics are kept until the account holder deletes the local files. Access can be withdrawn at any time in the TikTok app's settings, under the list of apps with permission to the account; the app's tokens then stop working.

5. This website

These pages set no cookies and run no analytics. They are hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect data from them.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.
